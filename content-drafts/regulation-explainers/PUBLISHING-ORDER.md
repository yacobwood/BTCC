## Why this cadence

- **Tuesday/Friday** spaces posts across the week without back-to-back publishing and gives each piece a few days of visibility before the next one lands.
- **2 a week is close to mandatory, not just a stylistic choice.** 48 articles at 1 a week would need 48 weeks, well past the actual 181-day gap between the last 2026 race and the first 2027 one. 2 a week fits with room to spare.
- **The Christmas/New Year break** (no posts Dec 23 - Jan 4) skips the two weeks readership is lowest, rather than burning content into a quiet news period.
- **Finishing April 6, four days before the season restarts**, avoids the series' final post landing right on top of season-opener coverage, while still running until pre-season buzz is realistically building.

## Two scheduling coincidences worth knowing about

- **what-it-costs-to-enter.md and team-setup-running-costs.md land March 9 and 12, 2027.** The 2026 season's entry deadline was noon on March 6th. If the 2027 season follows the same pattern, these two cost-focused articles will publish right as that year's real registration deadline is in the news, rather than as an arbitrary aside.
- **testing-rules-300km.md lands March 16, 2027.** The 2026 season's official pre-season test days ran March 24-25 (Croft) and April 8 (Season Launch/Test Day, Brands Hatch Indy). If 2027 follows a similar pattern, this article lands in the same run-up window as the real thing, rather than months removed from it.

Neither of these are confirmed 2027 dates, since TOCA hadn't published the 2027 entry deadline or test day schedule at the time this was written, but if the pattern holds from 2026, the timing works in the series' favour.
